Germany
Federal Parliamentary Republic · Updated October 2026

Germany was designed in 1949 by people who had watched a democracy get captured from within. So power is deliberately smeared across coalition partners, sixteen Länder, an assertive constitutional court, an independent central bank and a corporatist web of unions and employers. The formal map mostly works as advertised — the real story of 2026 is whether that consensus machine can survive a far-right party leading the national polls.
