Japan
Constitutional Monarchy · Parliamentary · Updated October 2026

On paper Japan is a textbook Westminster-style democracy. In practice one party, the Liberal Democratic Party, has governed for all but about four years since 1955, and power has long flowed through the triangle of LDP elders, the elite ministries of Kasumigaseki and big business. Sanae Takaichi's February 2026 landslide gave a prime minister rare personal authority — the question is how far she uses it on defence, the constitution and China.
