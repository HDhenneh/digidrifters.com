Вертикаль власти · Power Vertical · Updated October 2026

Russia's formal constitutional structure mirrors a conventional presidential republic, with elections, a bicameral legislature and a constitutional court. It isn't one in practice. Power runs through the 'vertical of power' (вертикаль власти) — personal loyalty to Vladimir Putin, enforced by the security services and rewarded through patronage, not through the institutions printed in the constitution. This atlas maps both layers: the legal architecture, which is largely decorative, and the real chain of command running through the Security Council, the siloviki clans and a managed political system that delivered a record 349-seat supermajority for the ruling party in September 2026's wartime election.
