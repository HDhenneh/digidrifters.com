European
Union
Supranational Union of 27 States · Updated October 2026

The EU is the bonus chapter because it breaks the template: there is no head of state, no government that can be voted out, and no single capital. Power is split between institutions that represent Europe (the Commission, Parliament, Court, ECB) and those that represent nations (the European Council and Council of Ministers). The formal rules are unusually detailed — but in practice the biggest capitals, the permanent diplomats of COREPER and the Commission president's inner circle settle most things before anyone votes.
